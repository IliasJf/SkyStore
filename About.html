Questions

Do I need an account to order?

No. Your name, phone number and address are enough. An account only keeps your order history.

How do I pay?

In cash, to the courier, when your parcel arrives. We never ask for your card.

How do I choose my size?

Every clothing and shoe page has a size guide with measurements in centimetres. Between two sizes, take the bigger one.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes. Reply to our confirmation call or write to us on WhatsApp before the parcel leaves.
