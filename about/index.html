Delivery & returns

Everything about getting your kit, and sending it back if it isn’t right.

Shipping
Free over $100. Otherwise a flat $8, wherever we deliver. Orders leave within 2 working days.
Delivery times
2 to 4 days in Europe and North America, 5 to 8 days elsewhere, with tracking by email.
Returns
30 days, any reason, unworn with tags. Start it from your account; the label is prepaid.
Size swaps
Free, once per order item. We send the new size as soon as the old one is scanned.
Care
Wash at 30°C, inside out, no softener: it blocks the fabric's wicking. Hang to dry.

Common questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders leave our warehouse within 2 working days and arrive in 2 to 4 days in Europe and North America, 5 to 8 days elsewhere. You get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships.

What does shipping cost?

Nothing on orders over $100 (or €92). Below that it's a flat $8 to anywhere we deliver.

Can I return something?

Yes, within 30 days, for any reason, as long as it's unworn with the tags on. Start a return from your account and we'll email a prepaid label. Refunds reach your card within 5 working days of the parcel arriving.

What if the size is wrong?

Swap it for free. Every product page has a size guide in centimetres and inches, and shoes list US, UK and EU sizes side by side.

Which payments do you take?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Card details go straight to the payment provider; we never see or store them. (This site is a demo: it only accepts the test card 4242 4242 4242 4242.)
